> [!WARNING] Retired graphic styles
> The previous Graphic Library (Filled, Schematic, Geometric, and What's New graphics) and spot illustrations are retired from brand and marketing work. Don't use, adapt, or recolor them. Replace them with a Resolve graphic when you refresh existing work.
